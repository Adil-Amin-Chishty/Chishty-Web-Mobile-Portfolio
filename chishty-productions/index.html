A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before we begin.

Can you handle design and development together?

Yes. A web or app project can include interface design and development in one scope. We agree on features, deliverables and responsibilities before starting.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We collaborate remotely, share progress online and agree on communication times that fit both time zones.

What does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and deliverables. Share your idea, deadline and budget range so we can discuss an appropriate proposal.

Do I need a finished brief?

No. Tell us the goal and what you have so far. For video work, bring your footage or reference style. For websites and apps, bring the problem you want to solve.
